# Glossary

**JP-JP meaning** (*znaczenie JP-JP*)
: The back of a card explained in Japanese, taken from the first monolingual dictionary in the user's ordered list that has the word. Words none of them has keep their English gloss. A note whose meaning came from that list carries the tag `J-J`.

**Monolingual chain** (*łańcuch słowników jednojęzycznych*)
: The user's ordered list of monolingual dictionaries asked for a JP-JP meaning, each one only for the words the ones before it lacked. A definition that is only a pointer to another spelling counts as lacking.

**Onomatopoeia** (*onomatopeja*)
: A word JMdict/Jitendex tags `on-mim` (onomatopoeic or mimetic) or one the surasura onomatopoeia dictionary lists. Covers sound words (擬音語/擬声語) and manner words (擬態語) alike, common ones such as ゆっくり included.
_Avoid_: mimetic word, sound effect (as separate categories)

**Tag label** (*etykieta tagu*)
: The human-readable name shown for a dictionary tag (`n` → noun / rzeczownik / 名詞). Its language follows the language of the meaning beside it: Japanese on a card with a JP-JP meaning or an onomatopoeia card, the card-label language (PL/EN) otherwise.

**Interface language** (*język interfejsu*)
: The language of the app's and the desktop program's own texts: Polish, English or Japanese. Independent of the study language and of the card-label language.

**Vocabulary card** (*fiszka słownikowa*)
: The standard note: the word on the front, its reading, meaning, pitch and kanji on the back.

**Grammar point** (*punkt gramatyczny*)
: A looked-up word that the Bunpro dictionary lists and that the main dictionary tags as grammar (particle, conjunction, auxiliary, expression) or does not list at all. An ordinary noun or adverb Bunpro merely also lists is not one. Only the selection itself is judged, never the sentence around it.

**Context card** (*fiszka kontekstowa*)
: The note an onomatopoeia or a grammar point gets *instead of* a vocabulary card: the sentence it was met in on the front with the word underlined; on the back a sentence translation the user writes in themselves (empty when created) and an explanation — a dictionary definition for an onomatopoeia, Bunpro's meaning, explanation and examples for a grammar point. One per word; a word with no usable sentence gets a vocabulary card instead. A tag says which kind it is.
_Avoid_: sentence card, cloze card, onomatopoeia card (as the name of the note type)

**Unlearned card** (*fiszka nienauczona*)
: A card that has never been reviewed and is not suspended. The only cards whose meaning may be rewritten to JP-JP after the fact.
